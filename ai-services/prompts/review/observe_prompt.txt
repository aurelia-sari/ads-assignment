OBSERVE step.

Below is the plan you produced and the evidence that was actually collected by
executing it. Compare them.

Report two labelled lists:

PASS:
- what the evidence confirms, each with the specific value that confirms it

ISSUE:
- what the evidence contradicts, or what the plan asked for that the evidence
  does not cover

If the evidence begins with CHECK RESULTS, those PASS, FAIL and WARN lines
were decided in code from the detail beneath them. Copy every FAIL and WARN
into ISSUE and every PASS into PASS, then add only what the detail shows
beyond them. Never mark a CHECK RESULTS line as "not shown by the evidence".

If a planned check could not be evaluated from this evidence, put it under
ISSUE as "not shown by the evidence", not under PASS. Do not credit a check
that the evidence is silent about.

Quote endpoint names, table names, service names, or file names exactly as they
appear in the evidence.
