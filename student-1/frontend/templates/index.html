Agents: plan, work, review, then you decide

The shared Multi-Agent Server runs three agents on your task. The Planner picks the evidence and sets acceptance criteria, the Worker answers from that evidence only (your trip data, read through MCP, and the knowledge base), and the Reviewer checks the answer. Nothing is released until you approve, correct, partially accept or reject it.
